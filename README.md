# AI WhatsApp & CRM Automation

> **Independent Portfolio Project** — A practical automation system for capturing leads from WhatsApp or forms, updating CRM records, automating follow-ups, moving opportunities through pipeline stages, and helping teams manage appointments more efficiently.

## 🎯 Project Goal

The goal of this workflow is to reduce manual lead handling and improve response speed by connecting WhatsApp communication with CRM automation.

This workflow is designed to capture incoming inquiries, update or create contact records, trigger follow-up messages, move leads through the sales pipeline, and support appointment reminders without repetitive manual work.

## ⚙️ Workflow Architecture

```text
WhatsApp Message / Lead Form
        ↓
Capture New Lead
        ↓
Validate Contact Details
        ↓
Create / Update CRM Contact
        ↓
AI Qualification / Intent Detection
        ↓
Assign Pipeline Stage
        ↓
Send Automated WhatsApp Follow-up
        ↓
Notify Sales Team / Owner
        ↓
Appointment Reminder / Re-engagement
        ↓
Update CRM Notes + Lead Status
